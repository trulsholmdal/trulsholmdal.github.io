01 / OVERVIEW

A public-finance question with business implications.

Norwegian wealth taxation raises important questions about firm ownership, capital allocation and the financial environment for growing businesses. The thesis, completed as part of a bachelor's degree in Business Administration at USN, focused on wealth tax and its impact on Norwegian growth companies.

The topic connects public economics with practical questions about company development and the financing conditions facing owners and entrepreneurs.

02 / CONCEPTUAL THEMES

Three questions at the intersection of taxation and growth.

This portfolio entry outlines key economic questions connected to the thesis topic, without attributing particular findings to the original paper.

Ownership and incentives

How might wealth taxation influence the decisions of owners of growth-oriented businesses?

Capital and liquidity

What is the conceptual relationship between an owner's tax obligations and a growing firm's financing needs?

Business development

What wider policy trade-offs arise when evaluating taxation and entrepreneurial growth?
